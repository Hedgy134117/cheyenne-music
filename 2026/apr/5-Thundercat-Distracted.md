a record with absolutely everything a good Thundercat album should have: spacey production and vocals, insanely agile and virtuosic grooves, and hilarious lyrics. It just so happens that this album also has one of the best and most well-utilized feature lists I’ve seen in a minute - ESPECIALLY “She Knows Too Much,” which features the late Mac Miller. Endlessly fun, and as smooth on the years as you would expect from Thundercat!
