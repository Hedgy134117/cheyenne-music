on this record, Ederra presents a chameleonic take on R&B that puts her right in line with other innovators of the genre, like Mk.gee and Dijon. With feather-light vocals and arrangements, she concocts a sound that feels absolutely heavenly - particularly on moments like the chorus of “Gemini Eyes.”
