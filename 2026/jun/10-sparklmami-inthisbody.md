short, sweet, and irresistible. In only 9 songs and 24 minutes, sparklmami manages to tap into an infectious blend of disco, funk, bedroom pop, and Brazillian jazz to create an album that feels like her own little world. Lush opener “no te vayas” practically oozes with retro pastiche, and its confidently-delivered, warm spoken word passages make it an incredible tone setter. The vibe only becomes more weird and wonderful with the jazziness of tracks like “fajas.”
