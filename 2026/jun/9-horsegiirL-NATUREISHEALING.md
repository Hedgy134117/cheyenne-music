possibly the most highly anticipated album of 2026 for me! I have been enamoured with horsegiirL ever since I discovered her through her utterly insane single “eat, sleep, slay, repeat” in 2024. While the lead single “an apple a day” showed the German musician in a similar lane, “NATURE IS HEALING” is a vaguely conceptual album with an omnivorous sound (from 80s new wave to 2010s EDM, and plenty of Y2K pop vibes in between) that solidifies horsegiirL as more than a novelty artist.
