a club-ready Argentinian album teeming with fun and absurdity. Definitely one of the more enjoyable results of the recent electroclash craze for me, with its tracks dealing in cartoonish violence and subverted hypersexuality. It might be pretty on-the-nose, but I really enjoy what it does - especially with Six Sex’s playful, confident delivery.
