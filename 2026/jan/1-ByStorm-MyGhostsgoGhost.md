I received this album with a lot of excitement, after being mind-boggled by Injury Reserve’s final project in 2022. Their first project since reforming as “By Storm” is a triumph through and through, showing the group experimenting with gentler, folktronica-influenced sounds. From the first track (“Can I Have You For Myself?”) showcases some of the most beautiful and acoustic sounds they’ve ever created, before exploding into a glitchy, disorienting waltz. This contrast between pastoral beauty and cacophony brings to mind artists like Xiu Xiu - a comparison that also stands when looking at instrumentation like the eerie, distorted violin on “Best Interest.” Coupled with the group’s lyrics about grief and personal strife, *My Ghosts go Ghost* can be a demanding album to unpack (even if it’s less apocalyptic than Injury Reserve’s prior work), but it’s undeniably poignant and interesting.
